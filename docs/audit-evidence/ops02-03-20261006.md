Shared consumers include ERP, Catalog, Catalog2, Kotiba/PostgREST, Leadlens and
Milanaweb. Tomarix has a separate PostgreSQL container on the backend VM; it
shares CPU/memory/storage resources but its connections do not use the ERP VM's

No pool-timeout or connection-ceiling markers were found in the checked ERP
slot logs (previous 24 hours), or in the latest 20,000 PostgreSQL log lines.

was available. Absence of an error marker does not prove zero queueing.

Closure requires an operator-confirmed busy window, checkout/queueing evidence,
and an agreed global budget covering every consumer. The shared client pool
ceilings were not fully established. Keep ERP's 48-connection bound and reserve
an explicit maintenance margin inside the remaining 49; do not raise workers,
pool sizes or PostgreSQL limits from this observation. A passing short sample
does not guarantee capacity under simultaneous worst-case demand.
